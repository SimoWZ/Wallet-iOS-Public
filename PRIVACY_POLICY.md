# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Wallet: Cards & Subscriptions** ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage

We believe ensuring the privacy of your data is paramount.
**We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal information.**

- **Local Storage:** All data entered into the App (loyalty cards, subscriptions, expenses) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData/CoreData technology.
- **iCloud Sync:** If enabled, your data syncs across your devices via your personal iCloud account. This data is encrypted by Apple and is not accessible to us.

## 2. App Permissions

### Camera

The App requests access to your device's camera solely for the purpose of **scanning barcodes** (QR codes, EAN, etc.) to add loyalty cards. No images or video are recorded, transmitted, or stored on any server.

### Notifications

The App requests permission to send local notifications to remind you of upcoming subscription renewals. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

### Face ID / Touch ID

You may choose to protect access to the App using Biometric authentication (Face ID or Touch ID). This authentication process is handled entirely by the iOS system. The App only receives a notification of "success" or "failure" and never has access to your biometric data.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple's **StoreKit** for processing "Pro" version purchases. Financial transactions are handled entirely by Apple; we do not have access to your credit card information or billing details.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
